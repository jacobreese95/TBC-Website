Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

Temple Baptist Church (“TBC,” “we,” “us”) operates the public website for tbcwichita.com and related church digital tools, including our member church application. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use our website or app, we may collect:

  • Contact information you provide (name, email, phone)
  • Account information if you create a member profile in the church app
  • Ministry-related content you submit (messages, prayer requests, photos, calendar responses)
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device information needed to operate the service

How we use information

We use information to operate church ministries, communicate with members and visitors, improve our website and app, and maintain the security of our systems. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We may use trusted service providers (such as hosting, authentication, or giving platforms) to operate our digital tools. Those providers process data only to provide their services. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data security & retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information. Ministry records and account data are retained as needed for church operations and legal obligations.

Children

Our public website is general audience. Member app features are intended for church members and families under church supervision. Parents or guardians should oversee children’s use of church digital tools.
