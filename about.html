About Us

Welcome to Temple Baptist Church! Get to know us.

Who we are

We are an amazing church family! We have folks from all over, with all kinds of amazing stories. We would love to get to know you and learn your story.

Temple Baptist has been serving Wichita since 1950. We are an Independent Baptist church that desires to show the love of Christ to our community in and around Wichita, KS.

Statement of Faith

Tap a doctrine to read more.

The Bible

The Bible is the Holy Word of God. All sixty-six books are inspired by God and comprise the complete Word of God. God’s Word is preserved for English-speaking people in the King James Bible completed in 1611.

2 Timothy 3:16 · Psalm 119:89 · Psalm 12:6-7 · 1 Peter 1:25 · Hebrews 4:12

God the Father

There is one God. He is eternal, perfect, omnipotent, sovereign, omnipresent, Creator, and worthy of all worship. He is one God in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Deuteronomy 6:4 · Isaiah 45:5-7 · Matthew 28:19 · Revelation 4:11

God the Son — Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, fully God and fully man. He was born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died for our sins, rose bodily from the dead, and is coming again.

John 1:1-14 · Isaiah 7:14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3-4 · Acts 1:9-11

God the Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit convicts of sin, regenerates the believer, indwells, seals, and empowers Christians for holy living and service.

John 16:7-14 · Ephesians 1:13-14 · 1 Corinthians 6:19-20

Salvation

Salvation is by grace through faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ alone — not by works. All who repent and believe the gospel are born again and have everlasting life.

Ephesians 2:8-9 · John 3:16 · Romans 10:9-13 · Titus 3:5

The Church

The local church is a called-out assembly of baptized believers organized to preach the Word, observe the ordinances of baptism and the Lord’s Supper, and fulfill the Great Commission.

Matthew 28:18-20 · Acts 2:41-47 · Ephesians 4:11-16

Last Things

We hold a pre-tribulational, premillennial view of end-time events: the blessed hope of the rapture of the church, the tribulation, the return of Christ to reign, final judgment, and eternity with God for the redeemed.

1 Thessalonians 4:13-18 · Revelation 19–22 · John 14:1-3
